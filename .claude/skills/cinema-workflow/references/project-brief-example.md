# IT_MES_06

MyEdSpace math tutoring. 3:14, vertical 9:16, US market. VO explainer, no on-screen dialogue.

## ✨ Context

MyEdSpace — live online math tutoring, grades 4–12, US only. Meta / paid social, web funnel.

Adapted from a Savvy Kid parenting-app ad (3:15, VO explainer): the engine being tested is a
calm expert voice walking one mechanism end to end — symptom, why it really happens, why the
obvious fix makes it worse, objection, tool, close. No dialogue anywhere in the reference,
and that is the part worth copying.

This is a test of that engine on a math-gap angle, not of a new offer.

## 🎭 Narrative

**Audience.** US parents of grades 4–12 who believe their kid is not trying. They have
already tried sitting with them, and it did not work.

**Logline.** Most kids who fall behind in math didn't stop trying — they stopped
understanding, and everything built on top of that step has been unreadable ever since.

**Structure.**
1. Hook — the accusation the parent has already made in their head.
2. Mechanism — math is cumulative; one missed step makes the next unreadable.
3. Misreading — from across the room it looks like laziness.
4. The usual fix — another hour practising exactly what they cannot do.
5. Cost — the kid stops asking; silence reads as fine; the gap stays.
6. Objection — "so hire a tutor for every step they missed?" No.
7. Tool — a specialist teacher works the curriculum live, in order, nothing skipped.
8. Close — on the offer.

**Message.** It is a missing step, not a missing character.

**Voice.** Single VO, calm and unhurried, US English, third person throughout. Never
addresses the viewer's own child directly.

## 🎨 Visuals

**Look.** Domestic realism, undecorated. Muted greys, cream, warm wood. Real household mess
— papers, wrappers, backpacks. Evening interiors lit by one practical lamp; daytime
interiors flat and overcast. Grade shifts with the act: neutral through the problem,
warmer through the solution, black for the end cards.

**Camera.** Calm handheld throughout — operator breath, micro-settling, no gimbal glide, no
digital jitter. No push-in, no zoom, no dramatic tightening. Shot sizes stay in the
medium-to-close band; the camera observes and never comments.

**Casting.** Ordinary American families, no styling, no makeup, clothes that have been worn
before. Nobody is attractive on purpose.

**Do.** Unretouched skin with visible texture. Real weight in held objects. Natural fabric
and hair movement. Faces stay reactive even when silent.

**Don't.** No legible content on any screen. No character speaks on camera — mouths stay
closed, so no lip sync anywhere. No stock-photo smiling. No text in frame except the end
cards.

**Format.** 9:16. Burned-in captions, two lines, bottom third. Black end cards, white type.

## 🎬 Production

**Model and settings.** Higgsfield Cinema Studio 4 / Seedance. Generate at 480p; upscale is
optional and only after approval — ByteDance Upscale, Pro, 1080p, 30fps, preset Short Series.
Single-prompt maximum is 30 seconds, so any scene longer than that splits at a cut.

**Locations.** @KITCHEN evening · @BEDROOM night · @LIVING daylight · @DINING afternoon.
Generate each empty, without characters, before any scene.

**Cast.** @P1 mother 38 · @P2 father 42 · @P3 mother 35 · @K1 boy 11 · @K2 girl 13 ·
@K3 boy 14. Character sheet for each before generating any scene.

**Props.** @TEST · @WORKBOOK · @PENCIL · @LAPTOP · @PHONE · @BACKPACK.

**Pipeline.** Approved scenes go to the edit for trim, assembly, captions and sound. This
project stops at approved scene files.
