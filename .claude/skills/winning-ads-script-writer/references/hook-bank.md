## Generating new hooks — the matrix

Fill the grid, then write. One cell = one hook option. Aim for 6–9 cells, pick two.

| axis | choices (from measured winners) |
|---|---|
| **moment with a witness** | interview waiting room · first line of a meeting · Zoom call opener · small talk at a party/conference · being asked "tell me about yourself" · pitching to a board · asking for a raise · being talked over |
| **flinch type** | judgement said to you ("Your accent is strong") · your own tic caught ("Does that make sense?") · a rule broken ("Never, and I mean never…") · a bias ("Long flight?") · a routine confession ("6:30 a.m.…") |
| **reframe target** | not accent → articulation · not confidence → small-talk problem · not ADHD → untrained skill · not intelligence → how thoughts come out · not knowledge → how you sound saying it |
| **avatar** | 47-yr professional who goes quiet · fluent non-native judged on accent · (test only) founder pitching · (test only) creator on camera |
| **market** | Malaysia/SEA: nationality line allowed and personalizable · US: no accent/ESL line, universal workplace pain · UA/CIS: dating/social hooks have converted |

Pressure test each option against pattern-library.md sections A and F before it leaves the grid:
flinch with witness (P1) · reframe by 6 s (P2) · readable in silence (P16) · costs the character something (F2) · no stat (F4) · no brand in first 10 s (F9) · market-appropriate framing (F6) · not a verbatim live family (check Recipe 1 body lines).

Write new hooks into the bank only after they have run: add the code and the measured result, or move them to Losers.
