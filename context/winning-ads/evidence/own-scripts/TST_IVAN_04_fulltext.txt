\# Results Screen + Retake Loop — Spec (extension to the live roleplay prototype)

  

Scope: what happens AFTER \`session\_end\`. Two screens (Results, Retake Delta), one new server contour (Evaluator), one retake flow. Web prototype quality bar: functional and honest, not pretty.

  

Core principle: the screen must end in action, not in reading. Every problem shown is tappable and leads to a 30-second retake. If the user reads the report and closes the tab, the screen failed regardless of how correct the analysis was.

  

\---

  

\#\# 1. Evaluator contour (new, server-side)

  

A third async contour next to brain and supervisor. Runs on \`session\_end\` and after every retake.

  

\*\*Why it exists\*\*: the Cascade path optimizes for latency and only sees clean text. The Evaluator gets the RAW audio and video and listens like a human — articulation, intonation, swallowed endings, tension, congruence between voice and face. Latency is irrelevant here; completeness is everything.

  

\*\*Inputs\*\* (buffered during the session, held in memory/tmp only):

\- Raw PCM segments per user turn (16 kHz mono, as sent to STT)

\- Webcam frames (the existing 1 fps ring buffer, extended to keep the whole session in HARNESS-friendly chunks)

\- Scribe transcripts per turn (final, with word timestamps if the provider gives them)

\- Conversation history with Q\&A structure (which assistant turns were questions/interjections)

\- Session metadata: scenario, strictness, interjection log, barge-in log

  

\*\*Calls\*\* (Gemini Flash, multimodal, audio passed AS AUDIO, not as transcript):

1\. \*\*Perception pass\*\* — per user turn: \`{"heard\_transcript": "...", "unclear\_words":\[{word, t\_ms, reason}\], "delivery":\[{span\_t\_ms, pace\_wpm, energy:0-1, monotone:bool}\], "fillers":\[{word, t\_ms}\]}\`. Prompt instruction: "Transcribe what an average listener in a room would catch. If a word is slurred or swallowed, mark it unclear rather than guessing."

2\. \*\*Articulation delta\*\* — computed in code, no extra call: diff Scribe transcript (machine ears, better than human) vs Evaluator's \`heard\_transcript\` (human ears). Words Scribe got but the human-listener pass didn't = the articulation problem map. This diff is the single most defensible metric on the screen.

3\. \*\*Meaning retention\*\* — one call over the whole session: key points the user made (max 12) vs what a listener would retain, with a one-sentence "how a listener would retell this". For lost points, classify: \`never\_said | said\_but\_buried\` (with t\_ms and cause: monotone/rushed/energy\_drop).

4\. \*\*Q\&A verdicts\*\* — per question asked by the character: \`{question, verdict: "answered|dodged|floundered|owned\_not\_knowing", evidence\_quote, t\_ms}\`.

5\. \*\*Visual pass\*\* — frames + transcript: gaze split (camera/away/down %), max 3 concrete observations with t\_ms, congruence flags ("said 'absolutely sure' at 3:12, face tensed").

  

\*\*Output\*\*: one \`report.json\` per session (schema below), written to \`logs/\`, pushed to client as \`{"type":"report", ...}\`.

  

\*\*Audio retention rule\*\*: raw segments live only until the Evaluator finishes, then are deleted. What persists: metrics + up to 6 short proof clips (≤6 s each, audio or audio+frame) extracted for the UI, referenced by t\_ms.

  

\#\# 2. report.json schema (the contract between server and screen)

  

\`\`\`json

{

  "session\_id": "...", "scenario": "investor", "duration\_s": 312,

  "score": 71, "score\_prev": null,

  "verdict\_line": "Convincing content, delivery buried a third of it.",

  "attention\_pct": 68,

  "retention": {"points\_total": 12, "points\_kept": 7,

                                         {"type":"fillers","count":14,"top\_word":"like","timestamps":\[...\]}\]},

    "articulation": {"score": 74, "unclear\_words":\[{"word":"scalability","t\_ms":88000,"proof\_clip":"clip\_01"}\],

Hard rules enforced in code, not prompts:

\- \`improvements\` is EXACTLY 3: two \`kind:fix\` + one \`kind:keep\`. If the evaluator returns more fixes, rank by (retention impact, then ease of fix) and cut.

\- Every \`fix\` MUST have a \`proof\_clip\` and a \`retake\` object. No proof or no retake action → the item is dropped and the next-ranked one promoted.

\- Every claim with a t\_ms must be verifiable from the clip. The evaluator prompt says: "Only report what you can point to. No general advice."

  

\#\# 3. Results screen — four floors, one scroll

  

\*\*Floor 1 — verdict (visible without scrolling):\*\* score, \`verdict\_line\`, "Audience held: 68%", and \`Δ vs previous attempt\` when \`score\_prev\` exists (retakes and repeat sessions populate it). Nothing else.

  

\*\*Floor 2 — moments:\*\* two cards side by side: \*Best moment\* (plays clip\_00, one line why) and \*Biggest loss\* (the top fix's clip). Best moment first — the screen must not open as a punishment.

  

\*\*Floor 3 — breakdown:\*\* attention curve as a horizontal timeline; annotated events are tappable dots → tapping plays the proof clip and shows the cause line. Below: five channel rows (delivery / articulation / content / visual / Q\&A) with scores, each expanding to its issues. Articulation row always shows the Scribe-vs-human line verbatim — it's the most convincing sentence on the screen. Q\&A expands to the question list with verdict chips.

  

\*\*Floor 4 — "What to improve" (the point of everything):\*\* the three improvement cards. Each fix card: title, one-line evidence, play-proof button, and one primary button \*\*"Retake — 30 sec"\*\*. The keep card: title, evidence, play button, no CTA. Below the cards, one secondary action: "Run the full scenario again".

  

Copy rules for everything on screen: second person, direct, specific, no corporate padding, no em-dashes. "The business model didn't land" — not "There is an opportunity to enhance clarity in the monetization section."

  

\#\# 4. Retake flow

  

Tap "Retake" on a fix card →

  

\*\*Retake screen:\*\* camera on, the \`focus\` instruction as the only text on screen, a 30 s countdown (from \`retake.max\_s\`), auto-stop at zero or manual stop. If the fix references a Q\&A item, the character ASKS the question again by voice (reuse the TTS path) and then listens — the retake is a real mini-exchange, not a monologue into the void.

  

\*\*On stop:\*\* the segment goes through the SAME Evaluator (perception pass + articulation delta + retention check scoped to this one point). Target: delta on screen in \<10 s; show a lightweight "listening back…" state, never a spinner without words.

  

\*\*Delta screen (the reward):\*\* strictly comparative, three rows max, computed old vs new:

\- the metric that motivated the fix, as before → after ("fillers 5 → 1", "monotone 45 s → alive", "listener retell now includes the business model ✓")

\- two pitch/energy sparklines stacked, old flat vs new — visual, no numbers needed

\- verdict line: "This version lands." or honest "Still buried — the number needs a pause before it."

Buttons: \*\*"Once more"\*\* (primary, restarts the retake instantly) and "Back to results" (the fix card now shows a ✓ and the new mini-score). If the character asked the question, a successful delta also plays his \`nod\` beat on screen — the person he was losing is now with him.

  

\`report.json\` gets \`retakes:\[{imp\_id, attempt, delta:{...}, ts}\]\` appended; \`score\_prev\` logic uses the latest attempt.

  

\#\# 5. What to log (this decides go/no-go on the whole loop)

  

Per session, in the JSONL log: report generated (y/n, evaluator latency), which improvement cards were tapped, retakes started / completed / repeated ("Once more" count), time from report shown to first retake tap. The single success metric of the prototype loop: \*\*≥1 completed retake per session when testing on ourselves.\*\* If we don't retake our own pitches, users won't.

  

\#\# 6. Harness additions

  

\- \`report.jsonl\` scenario: feed a logged real session through the Evaluator; assert schema validity, exactly-3 improvements rule, every fix has proof\_clip + retake, clips exist on disk, raw PCM deleted after evaluator completion.

\- \`retake.jsonl\`: scripted 25 s retake segment; assert delta object computed against the referenced issue, \`Once more\` restart works, report append is correct.

\- MOCK\_LLM canned outputs for both evaluator passes so the screens can be built and tested before burning real multimodal calls.

  

\#\# 7. Build order

  

R1: Evaluator contour + report.json on a real logged session (no UI, read the JSON by hand). R2: Results screen floors 1–4 rendering that JSON, clips playable. R3: Retake flow + delta screen + logging. Manual gate after R3: run your real pitch, do one retake, decide if the loop feels worth repeating — that feeling is the acceptance criterion.
