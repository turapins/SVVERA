## Structural facts about how the team already writes (from the scripts themselves)
- Every long-form web creative is modular: HOOK1 (+HOOK2..4) → BODY1 ("How long does it take to articulate thoughts…" podcast Q&A, verbatim across 10+ scripts) → BODY2 (Daily Plan VO, verbatim) → CTA card "START SPEAKING CONFIDENTLY NOW (ARROW DOWN)". Only the hook block changes between creatives. This is why reworks are hook-only.
- The podcast Q&A body has been reused since at least KR_TST_55 (spring 2026) and is in every one of the top-7 Spiral-ranked Vocal Image App videos. It carries the objection handling (going blank / reading books / equipment) and the day-3/7/14 timeline.
- Two stat lines live in the shared VO: "93% of how you're perceived comes from how you communicate" (KR_TST_59 body, 88e79cf5, 96ca3c74) and "81% of how you're perceived…" (IT_TST_61_C body). Both are unverified and the 93% one is the Mehrabian myth re-skinned. They violate playbook.md and criteria.md and are live in two of the top ten ads.
- Time-to-result numbers in the body drift between uploads: 9 minutes / 10 minutes / 15 minutes a day, 21 / 28 days, "by the end of July" / "by September first" / "by May 28th". The dated deadline is re-cut per month.
- Casting/location variants are the primary multiplication lever: 3–9 output videos per script, one hook × N characters.
