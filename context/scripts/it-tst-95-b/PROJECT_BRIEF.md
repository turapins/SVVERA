# IT_TST_95_B

Vocal Image web-funnel ad, 9:16, ~2:35 master. "Elevator with the CEO" hook, live product demo, podcast body. SEA market first (Malaysia), three castings.

## ✨ Context

Vocal Image (AI speaking coach app, vocalimage.app). Web funnel: Meta "Learn more" → quiz. First creative written with the winning-ads-script-writer skill (2026-09-03). Tests two things against the measured winners: an own-stumble hook in a new moment (elevator, CEO) and a live record → feedback → re-record beat showing the product working (untested in our ads; BoldVoice/Patter prove this way). Body is the proven podcast Q&A + Daily Plan, unchanged.

## 🎭 Narrative

Audience — professionals 40+, Malaysia/SEA, smart, go quiet when it counts; problem-aware.
Logline — a senior analyst wastes his one minute with the CEO, trains articulation for nine minutes a day, and next time has the sentence before the question ends.
Structure — elevator stumble → mirror line → peer diagnosis → live demo on the phone → weeks-later testimony → podcast Q&A → Daily Plan VO → CTA card.
Message — it's not nerves or knowledge, it's untrained articulation, and it trains in nine minutes a day.
Voice — on-camera dialogue in English (US register), SEA-neutral accents; podcast body two-hander; Daily Plan is VO at the edit.

## 🎨 Visuals

Look — clean corporate realism, neutral daylight, muted greys and light wood, no film grain, no stylisation. Podcast studio warm key, dark backdrop.
Camera — locked or slow handheld; medium and medium-close; one side of the action line per scene; word-by-word centered captions added at the edit.
Casting rules — ordinary professionals, natural untouched complexion, ages read honestly (47 looks 47). Three castings per role across Malay / Chinese-Malaysian / Indian-Malaysian; the CEO reads ethnically neutral in each. Fully dressed office clothes; C1 has two wardrobes (light blue shirt scenes 1–4; different shirt scene 5).
Do — phone screens are OFF/dark in generation (the feedback UI is composited from the real app at the edit); nothing branded; whole head inside the frame on 9:16.
Don't — no Vocal Image logo or UI invented by the model; no text on screens; no talking to camera except scene 5; no mouth movement without a written line.
Format — 9:16, 480p generation, captions and end card at the edit.
